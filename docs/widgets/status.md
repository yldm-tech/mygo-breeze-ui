# Status

The widgets of [`ui/breeze/widgets_status.go`](../../ui/breeze/widgets_status.go), whose catalog entries are [`ui/breeze/catalog/status.json`](../../ui/breeze/catalog/status.json). What every widget shares, its platforms, themes and limits, is in [Breeze UI for MyGo](../breeze-ui.md).

## Looks

| Widget | macOS | Windows 11 | GNOME |
|---|---|---|---|
| `InfoBar` | a grouped form section (corners of 12, NSBox's fill) with a 13-point SF Symbol of the severity, bold title, secondary message and an xmark button (macOS has no message bar; inferred) | InfoBar: severity background and a 15-pixel icon, 48 high, 1-pixel card stroke over the window, message beside or below the title, 38-pixel close button | AdwBanner: full-width strip of #7d7d83 at 30%, bold centered title, Dismiss button at least 84 wide (banners have no severity) |
| `InfoBarActions` | an `InfoBar` with buttons at its end before the xmark, centered on the title's line, 8 after the text and 6.25 before the xmark's box, the row 4 points taller for a 24-point push button, the faces over the section's fill (measured from SwiftUI's buttons in that row) | InfoBar's ActionButton: 16 after the message and 8 from the top on its line, 12 below the message in a narrow bar, faces over the bar's color | AdwBanner's button (button-label) in place of Dismiss, a `PrimaryButton` as its suggested action |

## Sources

The status widgets of `widgets_status.go`, `InfoBar`, `ProgressRing` and `StatusPage`, are not MyGo's widgets: they are built from its primitives (rows, columns, `ui.ButtonBase`, `ui.Icon`, `ui.RichText` and painting), each with its test writing its screenshots (`TestScreenshotsStatus`):

- **macOS:** AppKit and SwiftUI rendered at 2x into an sRGB bitmap on macOS 27.2 by the status set of [`tools/appkit-reference`](../../tools/appkit-reference), at the places where the test puts Breeze UI's: NSProgressIndicator of style spinning with `isIndeterminate` false at the regular and small sizes (for the default blue its arc is #0a82ff in Aqua and darker in Dark Aqua, #1769e6, and #177be5 once full), SwiftUI's `ContentUnavailableView` in an NSHostingView, and, for `InfoBar`, a row of a SwiftUI grouped `Form` with a Label of an SF Symbol and a borderless xmark button, the closest look macOS has to a message bar, which it does not have: that look is inferred, its sizes and colors measured. Breeze UI's draws within a few pixels at 2x of them, its text lines a little shorter than SF's.
- **Windows 11:** microsoft-ui-xaml's main branch: `InfoBar_themeresources.xaml` and `InfoBar.xaml` (the inline bar's informational background is the translucent `SystemFillColorAttentionBackground`, where the floating toast's is the solid one; InfoBarPanel's two arrangements, the message beside the title or 4 below it, and a title without a message laid out as the second, as InfoBarPanel lays a single child), `ProgressRing_themeresources.xaml`, `ProgressRing.cpp` and the Lottie of `AnimatedVisuals/ProgressRingDeterminate.cpp` (an ellipse of radius 8 scaled 1.77 on a 32-unit canvas, its stroke 1.5 scaled the same, trimmed to the value through the Lottie's keyframes, over a transparent track). WinUI has no empty-state control: `StatusPage` follows its type ramp and is inferred; only its text styles were compared, with the TextBlock styles of `winui-text`. [`tools/winui-reference`](../../tools/winui-reference/Program.cs) renders InfoBars of every severity with a message (`winui-status`) and without one (`winui-status-titles`, which `TestScreenshotsStatus` matches with `statusTitlesView`) and determinate rings of 25, 60, 100 and 0 percent at 32 DIPs and of 60 at 16 at the test's places, at 2x on Windows 11 26H2, light and dark. The rings, the bars' layout (the title 14 below the top, the message 12 after it, a title alone laid out vertically, 53 high with Segoe UI Variable), their backgrounds and the close button's place match; the comparison corrected the bar's border, `CardStrokeColorDefault` over the window rather than over the bar's color (229 and 29 around every severity), the severity icon, a 15-DIP circle a DIP below its box's top with smaller marks in 1-DIP strokes, drawn from the render, and the close button's ×, 11 DIPs square. The informational bar's `SystemFillColorAttentionBackground` reads 244 where Breeze UI's mix reads 245.
- **GNOME:** libadwaita 1.10.0's `_toolbars.scss` and `adw-banner.c` for the banner (its three arrangements by width: the title centered across the strip, at the start, or the button below it), `_misc.scss` and `adw-status-page.ui` for the status page, and `_labels.scss` for `.title-1`, `.heading` and `.body`. Banners have no severity: libadwaita's `.error`, `.warning` and `.success` classes recolor only accent-colored parts, which a banner's title is not, so every kind of `InfoBar` draws the same banner; a banner has only a title, and the message follows it in the same label at regular weight (inferred). libadwaita has no determinate ring: `ProgressRing` takes AdwSpinner's track and stroke width with GtkProgressBar's accent (inferred). [`tools/adwaita-reference`](../../tools/adwaita-reference/reference.py)'s `status` and `statuspage` sets render AdwBanners (their titles in markup, the message at regular weight, as Breeze UI draws it), AdwSpinners where the rings are and an AdwStatusPage at the test's places, light and dark, with GTK 4.24.1, and Breeze UI's match them but for the glyphs: the banners' strips (213 in the light theme, 61 in the dark one), their 46-pixel height with the Dismiss button and its face (196 and 81, Breeze UI's 80), the title 12 in from the strip's start when it does not fit centered; the spinner's track, a ring of 4 pixels at 32 and 2 at 16 in the text color at 15% (221 and 67, Breeze UI's 220 and 67), whose arc only the accent replaces; and the status page's 128-pixel icon at 55% (140 and 155), its title, its description 12 below it on a 140% line and the button 36 below that, the content centered. Whether a banner's title fits centered depends on its width in the font: with Adwaita Sans "Informational Some message about it." does not, and libadwaita puts it at the start, where with DejaVu Sans it does. A banner without a button is as high as its title's line, which GTK rounds up to a whole pixel (42 where Breeze UI's is 41.5 with the host's font), and so is the status page's title (33).

`InfoBarActions` puts the actions where each platform puts a message bar's action. On macOS, which has none, `appkit-reference`'s `statusActionsSet` renders SwiftUI `Button`s after the `Spacer` of that grouped form row, a push button and a prominent one, with and without the xmark and in a bar 280 points wide, where `TestScreenshotsStatus` places Breeze UI's (`statusActionsView`), light and dark: the 24-point button is 10 points below the row's top, its middle on the title line's, which moves the symbol, the text and the xmark 4 points down and makes the row 4 points taller; it ends 6.25 points before the xmark's 16-point box, or 10 from the row's end without it; and its face is `secondarySystemFill` over the section's fill, 228 over 247 and 54 over 37, which `macOver` gives the buttons by building them in the fill's theme. Breeze UI's match it within a pixel at 2x but for the row's height, which MyGo's shorter line makes a point less. The 8 points before the actions and between them are HStack's spacing, inferred: a message that fits beside a button in the render leaves 10. On Windows the ActionButton follows `InfoBar.xaml` and `InfoBar_themeresources.xaml` (the panel's third child, `InfoBarActionHorizontalOrientationMargin` 16,8,0,0 and `InfoBarActionVerticalOrientationMargin` 0,12,0,0) and `InfoBarPanel.cpp` (the panel goes vertical when its children with their margins are wider than it or taller than `InfoBarMinHeight`, and lays out two children, a title and an action, across); its buttons take their translucent faces over the bar's color (`fluentOver`), and they sit in a row at least 32 DIPs high, so that a `Link` lines up with the title as InfoBar's HyperlinkButton does (inferred). On GNOME the actions take the place of AdwBanner's button (`button-label`, with `button-style` suggested for a `PrimaryButton`, libadwaita 1.7) in the banner's three arrangements, their faces the Dismiss button's over the strip. [`tools/winui-reference`](../../tools/winui-reference/Program.cs)'s `winui-status-actions` scene and [`tools/adwaita-reference`](../../tools/adwaita-reference/reference.py)'s `statusactions` set render WinUI's and libadwaita's counterparts at the test's places; they were not run for this comparison, so the Windows and GNOME placements are taken from the sources and inferred.

`InfoBar` and the banner pick their arrangement from the width the bar had in the previous frame, as the platforms' layouts pick it from the width they are given; a bar shown for the first time is laid out once more at its own width, and after a resize it follows a frame later. `ProgressRing` fills clockwise from 12 o'clock in both reading directions, as a clock does; a negative value shows `Spinner`.

## Limits

Besides [those of every widget](../breeze-ui.md#limits):

- **Message bar actions.** `InfoBarActions` places the actions by their size in the previous frame, as a bar picks its arrangement by its width in the previous frame: an action shown for the first time is laid out once more, and an action that changes size moves a frame later. AdwBanner has one button, which the actions replace: on GNOME a closable bar with actions shows no Dismiss button, and an action closes it by setting `*open` to false; and the buttons keep their own widths, where AdwBanner makes its button at least 84 pixels wide, 160 below the title. WinUI's InfoBar has one ActionButton too: several actions sit 8 DIPs apart (6 pixels on GNOME, 8 points on macOS), inferred.
