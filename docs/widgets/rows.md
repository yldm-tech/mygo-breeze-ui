# Rows

The widgets of [`ui/breeze/widgets_rows.go`](../../ui/breeze/widgets_rows.go), whose catalog entries are [`ui/breeze/catalog/rows.json`](../../ui/breeze/catalog/rows.json). What every widget shares, its platforms, themes and limits, is in [Breeze UI for MyGo](../breeze-ui.md).

## Looks

| Widget | macOS | Windows 11 | GNOME |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Card` | SwiftUI's GroupBox: a grouped section's fill, corners of 12, padded 5 | the card of Expander and SettingsCard: `CardBackgroundFillColorDefault`, 1-pixel `CardStrokeColorDefault`, corners of 4, padded 16 | `.card`: `card_bg_color`, corners of 12, the card shadow, padded 12 (inferred) |
| `ActionRow` | row of a grouped form (System Settings): 10 in, 13-point title over an 11-point secondary subtitle, controls centered on the title's line; in a group, separators inset 10 with a point of their own | SettingsCard: at least 68 high, padded 16, 14-pixel header, 12-pixel secondary description, content 24 after them, or below them, 8 apart, at the start, in a card narrower than 476; in a `Card` or an `AccordionItem` a SettingsExpander's item, 52 high, a line above it, in an `AccordionItem` padded 58 at the start and 44 at the end | AdwActionRow: at least 54 high, header 12 in, dimmed smaller subtitle 3 below the title; in a group a boxed list with `card_shade_color` lines, a row with a line 55 high |
| `SwitchRow` | the row with a mini switch, 36 by 16, the row one switch for assistive technology | SettingsCard with a compact ToggleSwitch and its On or Off text 12 before it, in a column as wide as the wider of the two; the card under the pointer and pressed as a clickable SettingsCard | AdwSwitchRow: the row toggles on a click anywhere, 3% under the pointer, 8% pressed, its focus ring across its edge |

## Sources

`Card`, `ActionRow` and `SwitchRow` have no counterpart in package `ui` and are built of its elements: a card is a column, an action row a row that assistive technology sees as a group named by its title and described by its subtitle, and a switch row is `ui.SwitchBase` itself, so a click anywhere on it, Space and Enter toggle it, Tab stops at it once and assistive technology sees one switch named by the title, as AdwSwitchRow is one. Rows know the `Card`, `Fieldset` or `AccordionItem` they are built in and join as the platform joins rows there; built elsewhere, each is drawn as a group of one row.

On Windows they are compared with CommunityToolkit's own SettingsCards and SettingsExpander (CommunityToolkit.WinUI.Controls.SettingsControls 8.2.251219 over Windows App SDK 2.6.5), which [`tools/winui-reference`](../../tools/winui-reference/Program.cs) renders at 2x on Windows 11 26H2, light and dark, laid out as `TestScreenshotsRows` lays out Breeze UI's on Windows (`winui-rows`: a Border with the card's resources holding text, the card of rows as SettingsCards with DefaultSettingsExpanderItemStyle's setters, SettingsCards alone and 4 apart under a section header, a disabled one, and a SettingsExpander), and wider than 476 DIPs (`winui-rows-wide`). The card's fill and stroke (251 and 229, 43 and 29), the header and description (16 inside the border, the description right below the header, in `TextFillColorSecondary`), the content 24 after them and 17 from the card's end, centered on the card, a card's height around its content (70 with a ToggleSwitch), the ToggleSwitch's track and knob and the items' lines match. The comparison corrected what a SettingsCard narrower than `SettingsCardWrapThreshold` (476) does: it wraps, its content below the header and the description, 8 below them, at its start; the On and Off text of a ToggleSwitch, which share a column as wide as the wider; and the padding of a SettingsExpander's items, 58 at the start and 44 at the end, which an `AccordionItem`'s rows now take. The comparison with `winui-settings` (below) found the line between the rows of a `Card` and what a card's rows hold drawn over the window's color rather than over the card's fill (the line 229 where WinUI's reads 236, a Button's face 251 where WinUI's reads 254), which it corrected for these rows too. Still different: the text's line height (19 DIPs with Segoe UI Variable, the host's font's in Breeze UI), which makes WinUI's cards a few DIPs taller; the `AccordionItem`'s header, MyGo's, where a SettingsExpander's is a 70-DIP SettingsCard with its chevron in a 32-DIP button, filled 45 in the dark theme where the card reads 43, and whose first item shows 50 DIPs of fill below the header's line, where Breeze UI's first row shows 51; and a disabled card, which WinUI keeps at full strength with its text in `TextFillColorDisabled` (160 and 120) and its ToggleSwitch gray (197 with a white knob, 76 with a 171 knob), where MyGo fades the whole row to half opacity (see Limits).

On GNOME, `reference.py`'s `rows` set renders AdwActionRow and AdwSwitchRow in boxed lists, an AdwPreferencesGroup and an AdwExpanderRow, and a `.card`, where `TestScreenshotsRows` places `ActionRow`, `SwitchRow`, `Fieldset`, `AccordionItem` and `Card`, light and dark, with GTK 4.24.1 and libadwaita 1.10.0. The card, the rows (55 pixels high, 54 after a line, their titles 12 in, the subtitle 3 below the title), their lines, the switches, the button and the dimmed value match it to the pixel but for the glyphs, a text line shorter than GTK's whole pixels moving what follows by half a pixel. The comparison corrected the AdwPreferencesGroup: a group with a title and no description has a 34-pixel header with the title centered in it (box.single-line in 1.10.0's stylesheet) and its list 6 below it, 40 below the group's top, where Breeze UI put the list 12 below the legend; the lines between an expander row's nested rows, card_shade_color over the nested list (229 and 27) rather than over the card (237 and 33, the line below the header's); and a button's face, which is translucent in libadwaita ($button_color, currentColor at 10%), so that a button in a row shows the card through it (235 and 72) where Breeze UI's was the window's mix (230 and 56). In a `Form` a `Fieldset` follows the field above it by 24 pixels (ui.Form's gap and the 12 a fieldset keeps from what is above it, as an AdwPreferencesPage spaces its groups), which `reference.py`'s `layout` set now gives its AdwPreferencesGroup too; there the card's top matches the group's list to the pixel, and the card is 4.5 pixels taller than the AdwEntryRow it stands for, as it holds a label and an entry. What still differs: an AccordionItem's header is MyGo's, 37.5 pixels high where AdwExpanderRow's is 55, so the nested rows sit higher; a disabled row alone fades its card, 253 where GTK's is 255 and 43 in the dark theme where it is 51 (see Limits), and its checked switch's slider, faded by MyGo on its own over the faded track, reads (204, 224, 248) and (152, 171, 196) where GTK, fading the switch as a whole, keeps it white over the card (255, and 153 in the dark theme); the dark theme's card is 52 where GTK's is 51, as GTK rounds the card's 8% to a whole level of alpha.

## Limits

Besides [those of every widget](../breeze-ui.md#limits):

- **Settings rows.** On Windows a `SwitchRow` toggles on a click anywhere on its card, as AdwSwitchRow does, where Windows Settings toggles only on its ToggleSwitch, and its On and Off text is English, as Breeze UI cannot read MyGo's translations. Content other than rows built with rows in a `Card`, a `Fieldset` or an `AccordionItem` sits in the list without its padding: build rows alone there. Rows join the `Card`, `Fieldset` or `AccordionItem` whose fn builds them even inside another element there, such as a `Collapsible` or a `ui.Row`: build them directly in it. A disabled row alone fades its own box with its content at MyGo's half opacity, where AppKit and libadwaita keep the section or the boxed list as it is and dim only the row's content (SwiftUI's grouped form even keeps the title's color, measured on macOS 27.2). The padding of a GNOME card without rows, the gap between the controls at a row's end on macOS and Windows and the inset of the rows of a `Card` on Windows (16, as a Card has neither a SettingsExpander's icon nor its chevron) are inferred; on Windows rows narrower than 476 DIPs put their controls below their text, as SettingsCard does, deciding it from their width in the previous frame.
