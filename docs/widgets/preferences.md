# Preferences

The widgets of [`ui/breeze/widgets_preferences.go`](../../ui/breeze/widgets_preferences.go), whose catalog entries are [`ui/breeze/catalog/preferences.json`](../../ui/breeze/catalog/preferences.json). What every widget shares, its platforms, themes and limits, is in [Breeze UI for MyGo](../breeze-ui.md).

## Sources

`PreferencesGroup`, `PrefixRow`, `ContentRow` and `RadioRow` (`widgets_preferences.go`, with `TestScreenshotsPreferences`) are the rest of a settings page. A preferences group is a column that assistive technology sees as a group named by its title and described by its description, its rows joining as a `Fieldset`'s do (`rowsInFieldset`); the three rows are built on `ActionRow`'s row look, a prefix row and a content row as groups named by their title, a radio row as `ui.RadioBase` itself, so that a click anywhere on it, Space and Enter choose it and assistive technology sees one radio button named by the title, described by the subtitle and checked while chosen. Up and Down on a radio row choose the radio row before or after it among those of the same `Card`, `Fieldset`, `AccordionItem` or `PreferencesGroup` that choose into the same value, round from the last to the first and passing over disabled ones, and move the focus there (`HandleInput`, as MyGo's focus groups cannot leave out the controls a row holds at its end). Their values come from:

- **macOS:** SwiftUI's grouped `Form`, which `appkit-reference`'s preferences scene renders at 2x in Aqua and Dark Aqua on macOS 27.2 where `TestScreenshotsPreferences` places Breeze UI's widgets (`appkit-preferences-*.png`): a `Section` whose header is an `HStack` of a title and a `Button` and whose footer is a `Text`, holding rows whose label is a `Label` with a 30-point image, a row with a value, a `Picker` of the radio-group style whose options have a subtitle, and a `VStack` row of a title over a `TextEditor`. Breeze UI's match it but for the text (the host's font) and the text area, which is `TextArea`'s bezeled field where `TextEditor` draws none: the header and its button, the section 10 below them, the description's place and color (128 over the window in Aqua, 154 in Dark Aqua), the image's place, the separators running under it, the radio buttons at the rows' trailing edge with their trough over the section (223 over 247, 58 over 37), and the content row's label and content; `TestMacPreferencesMatchAppKit` checks the section's place, the image, the description and the trough. macOS 27.2 draws a radio-group picker in a grouped form as a row per option with the radio button at its end, which a `RadioRow` follows, rather than `Radio`'s button before its label.
- **Windows 11:** CommunityToolkit/Windows's `SettingsCard.xaml` and `SettingsCard.cs` at main a784bad: SettingsCardHeaderIconMaxSize 20 and SettingsCardHeaderIconMargin 2,0,20,0 for the prefix, the RightWrapped state that keeps the HeaderIcon beside the header and puts the content in the header's column below it, the RightWrappedNoIcon state below SettingsCardWrapNoIconThreshold, 286, and the Vertical ContentAlignment state with SettingsCardVerticalHeaderContentSpacing 8 for a content row; the WinUI Gallery's settings page for a group's header. A radio row is a clickable SettingsCard with a RadioButton (`Radio`'s, `fluentRadioDot`) as its HeaderIcon. None of it is compared with WinUI's rendering: `tools/winui-reference` needs Windows.
- **GNOME:** libadwaita 1.10.0's `adw-preferences-group.ui` and `adw-preferences-group.c` (the header box holding the labels and the header suffix, single-line while there is no description), `_preferences.scss` (border-spacing 6 in the group and its labels, the header's margin-bottom of 6 when it is not single-line, its min-height of 34 when it is), `adw-action-row.ui` and `_lists.scss` (the prefixes box, its margin of 6 and the header's border-spacing of 6). A radio row is an activatable AdwActionRow with a grouped GtkCheckButton (`adwRadioDot`) as its prefix, the pattern libadwaita documents. None of it is compared with libadwaita's rendering: `reference.py` needs GTK 4.24 and libadwaita 1.10.

## Limits

Besides [those of every widget](../breeze-ui.md#limits):

- **Preferences rows.** Each `RadioRow` is a Tab stop of its own, where a radio group is one: MyGo has no way to make elements one stop without a `FocusGroup` around them, which would take in the controls a row holds at its end; Up and Down move only among the radio rows of one group sharing a value, and a radio row alone takes no arrows. A focus moved by an arrow after a click shows no ring, as `HandleInput` cannot mark the focus as the keyboard's. On macOS a row of a prefix and two lines is 51 points high where SwiftUI makes it 52, its labels and its prefix a half point higher. On Windows the prefix is laid at its own size where SettingsCard's Viewbox scales a HeaderIcon to 20 DIPs, so build it 20 DIPs; a radio row keeps its radio button where a narrow SettingsCard would drop a HeaderIcon. `PreferencesGroup` is not lined up with the fields of a `Form`, as `Fieldset` is. The Windows and GNOME looks of the four are taken from the sources above, not compared with WinUI's and libadwaita's renders, and a group's description on Windows, a content row's spacing on GNOME and a radio row on Windows are inferred.

## What MyGo would need

Besides [what every widget would need](../breeze-ui.md#what-mygo-would-need):

- **Radio rows.** A focus group that leaves out the elements inside its members, or a way to make an element no Tab stop of its own while a sibling sharing its value is chosen, so that `RadioRow`s are one stop as a radio group is; and a way for `HandleInput` or `Focus` to show the focus as the keyboard's.
