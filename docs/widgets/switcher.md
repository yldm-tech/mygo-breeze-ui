# View switchers

The widgets of [`ui/breeze/widgets_switcher.go`](../../ui/breeze/widgets_switcher.go), whose catalog entries are [`ui/breeze/catalog/switcher.json`](../../ui/breeze/catalog/switcher.json). What every widget shares, its platforms, themes and limits, is in [Breeze UI for MyGo](../breeze-ui.md).

## Looks

| Widget | macOS | Windows 11 | GNOME |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ViewSwitcher` | in a `Toolbar` or `HeaderBar`, NSToolbarItemGroup of selectionMode selectOne: one 36-point platter of 37-point image segments (title segments without icons), the chosen one a pill 28 points high in a pressed item's gray, separators 1 by 20 between two unchosen ones; elsewhere NSSegmentedControl, each 16-point icon 2 points before its label | SelectorBar: items 40 high padded 12,10,12,7, the 16-DIP icon 8 before the text, the 16 by 3 accent pill under the chosen one, no fill under the pointer or pressed | AdwViewSwitcher with the wide policy: flat buttons 34 high, 3 apart, as wide as the widest and at least 120, the 16-pixel icon 6 before the bold label, the chosen one currentColor at 10% |

## Sources

`ViewSwitcher` (`widgets_switcher.go`, with `TestScreenshotsViewSwitcher`) has no counterpart in package `ui`: it switches an app's top-level views, each a `ViewItem` of an icon and a label, the chosen one `*selected`, and the caller builds the chosen view after it, as with `Tabs`. On macOS it is `ui.SegmentedBase`, a radio group of radio buttons, as AppKit reports an NSToolbarItemGroup and an NSSegmentedControl (AXRadioGroup); on Windows and GNOME `ui.TabsBase`, a tab list of tabs, as AdwViewSwitcher is (`GTK_ACCESSIBLE_ROLE_TAB_LIST`), where WinUI's SelectorBarItem is a list item with the selection pattern (`ItemContainerAutomationPeer`), which MyGo has no base for. Tab stops once, at the chosen item, the arrows choose among the items, mirrored right to left, and each item is named by its label, also where only its icon shows. Its values come from:

- **macOS:** the switcher scene of [`appkit-reference`](../../tools/appkit-reference), which renders at 2x on macOS 27.2 a titled window with a unified toolbar titled Music holding an NSToolbarItemGroup of selectionMode selectOne after a flexible space, and in its content an NSSegmentedControl of the same three segments, each an image and a label, both of the test's SVG icons as template images, where `TestScreenshotsViewSwitcher` places Breeze UI's (`appkit-switcher-*.png`), offscreen and, with `-live`, captured from a window ordered front without activating the app (`appkit-switcher-*-live.png`). The group is one 36-point platter of a bordered toolbar item; its segments are 37 points wide around a 16-point image, max(37, the image's width + 17) for 8- to 32-point images, or the widest title's advance plus 23.25 (at least 35) for titles, as NSToolbarItemGroups of template images and of titles laid out in the same toolbar measure. The chosen segment's pill is 28 points high, 4 inside the platter's top and bottom, 4 points in from the platter's start in the first segment and from its end in the last, and otherwise 3 after the boundary before its segment and 2 before the one after it (pills at 4 to 35, 40 to 72 and 77 to 107 points into a 111-point group of three, the same insets in groups of two to five images, of 24-point images and of titles), and each image or title is centered on its segment's pill rather than the segment, chosen or not; the pill reads 232 on the live platter's 252 in Aqua and (91,90,90) on (71,70,70) in Dark Aqua, a pressed toolbar item's face (black at 7.8%, (89,88,88)), which the offscreen render draws darker (204 on 251), with or without a key window's look. A separator 1 by 20 points stands at the boundary between two unchosen segments, black at 20% over the platter in Aqua and white at 28% in Dark Aqua (live). The NSSegmentedControl puts each 16-point image 2 points before its label: three segments of image and label are 18 points wider each than of labels alone. Breeze UI's group lays out as the render's, its platter, segments, pill and icons on the same pixels at 2x, with the live capture's fills (the comparison corrected the pills and icons of the middle and last segments, which had been spread evenly a point off AppKit's); the window's title and the control's labels are in the host's font.
- **Windows 11:** WinUI's SelectorBar on microsoft-ui-xaml's main branch (`controls/dev/SelectorBar`): `SelectorBar.xaml` (the ItemsView in a horizontal StackLayout, padded by SelectorBarPadding; the SelectorBarItem template's StackPanel of the icon and the text spaced SelectorBarItemSpacing 8, the icon's ContentPresenter scaled by SelectorBarItemIconScale 0.8 with SelectorBarItemIconVisualMargin -2,0, so that an IconElement of 20 takes 16 by 20 DIPs, and the pill in a row of its own under them), `SelectorBar_themeresources.xaml` (SelectorBarPadding 0,4, SelectorBarItemPadding 12,10,12,7, the foregrounds TextFillColorPrimary, Secondary under the pointer and Tertiary pressed, the pill 4 by 3 scaled to 16, and the SelectorBarItemBackground brushes, transparent in every state: WinUI fills no item under the pointer or pressed) and `SelectorBarItemAutomationPeer.cpp` (an item named by its Text). [`winui-reference`](../../tools/winui-reference/Program.cs)'s `winui-switcher` scene places a TitleBar holding a SelectorBar as its RightHeader and a SelectorBar alone where the test places Breeze UI's, rendered at 2x with WinUI 3 on Windows 11 26H2, light and dark: compared pixel by pixel, the bar's place at the end of the 374-DIP room before the drag column, the items' tops and heights (4 and 40), the icons' 16-DIP boxes 12 in and the text 36 in, the pill (16 by 3 DIPs at the item's bottom, #005a9e and #76b9ed) and the text and icon colors (26 and white over the window) matched, and nothing needed correcting. What still differs is the text, in the host's font, which changes the items' widths (90, 93 and 84 DIPs in Segoe UI Variable) and so where each item and its pill stand, and the icons, the test's SVGs where the scene uses Segoe Fluent Icons' glyphs.
- **GNOME:** libadwaita 1.10.0's AdwViewSwitcher: `adw-view-switcher.c` (a homogeneous GtkBoxLayout, the tab-list role, the policy, narrow by default and wide in a header bar as its documentation advises), `adw-view-switcher-button.ui` and `adw-view-switcher-button.c` (a `.flat` GtkToggleButton with the tab role holding a `.wide` box of the icon and the label, and MIN_NAT_BUTTON_WIDTH_WIDE 120), `_view-switcher.scss` (`viewswitcher`'s border-spacing of 3 and min-height of 34, the buttons' padding of 0, the `.wide` box's padding 2px 12px and border-spacing 6px) and `_buttons.scss` with `_colors.scss` (%button_basic_flat's fills: currentColor at 7% under the pointer, 16% pressed, 10%, 13% and 19% checked; a button's bold label and corners of 9). [`reference.py`](../../tools/adwaita-reference/reference.py)'s `switcher` set places an AdwHeaderBar with a wide AdwViewSwitcher packed at its end and one alone where the test places Breeze UI's. Rendered with GTK 4.24.1 and libadwaita 1.10.0 and compared pixel by pixel, light and dark, they match: the buttons 120 by 34 and 3 apart at the bar's end (148 to 514, 6 from the close button) and alone (16, 63), the content box centered in them, the chosen one's fill (230 over 250, 57 over 34), its corners of 9 and the text's color; the labels differ in the fonts' glyphs, sitting about a pixel lower in Breeze UI's, and the icons are the set's adwaita-icon-theme ones (folder-music, view-grid, network-wireless), not the test's SVGs. The bar's title sits 6 before the switcher, as GtkCenterBox moves AdwWindowTitle aside for an end box wider than half the room left. The comparison corrected the title's width: AdwWindowTitle's label asks for 5 characters (width-chars 5), so "Music", 43.6 wide in Adwaita Sans Bold, is centered in a label 74 wide (68 to 142), its ink from 84 to 126, where Breeze UI's title had been as wide as its text, 4 pixels further right; titles centered across the bar or wider than 5 characters do not move.

## Limits

Besides [those of every widget](../breeze-ui.md#limits):

- **View switchers.** `HeaderBar` takes its title as text, so a `ViewSwitcher` goes among its end controls, where Finder puts its view switcher in a toolbar and WinUI a TitleBar's RightHeader, while libadwaita apps put AdwViewSwitcher in place of the title (the header bar's title-widget), centered across the bar; a `HeaderBar` taking a title builder would allow that. AdwViewSwitcher's narrow policy (the icon over a smaller label, its default outside a header bar), AdwViewSwitcherBar at the bottom of a narrow window and the needs-attention dot and badge are not drawn, nor does the switcher change with the window's width, which apps do with an AdwBreakpoint. GTK enters an AdwViewSwitcher at the chosen button too but then moves among its buttons with Tab and the arrows without choosing; Breeze UI keeps MyGo's single stop, the arrows choosing. An NSToolbarItemGroup collapses into a pop-up button when the toolbar runs out of room, where MyGo's toolbar moves it into its own overflow menu. On macOS the title segments of a toolbar group are all as wide as the widest, where AppKit makes them a few points narrower when the widest is not the last; the pill of a single segment and of a pressed segment and the focus ring around the focused segment's pill are inferred. A disabled switcher takes MyGo's half opacity, where WinUI turns the text TextFillColorDisabled and the pill AccentFillColorDisabled.

## What MyGo would need

Besides [what every widget would need](../breeze-ui.md#what-mygo-would-need):

- **View switchers.** A base for a row of selectable items with one Tab stop that reports a list of list items with a selection, as WinUI's SelectorBar does (its items are ItemContainers, which `ItemContainerAutomationPeer` reports as ListItem with the SelectionItem pattern), where `TabsBase` reports tabs; and a way for a toolbar item to draw itself collapsed when the toolbar overflows, as an NSToolbarItemGroup becomes a pop-up button.
