# Settings rows

The widgets of [`ui/breeze/widgets_settings.go`](../../ui/breeze/widgets_settings.go), whose catalog entries are [`ui/breeze/catalog/settings.json`](../../ui/breeze/catalog/settings.json). What every widget shares, its platforms, themes and limits, is in [Breeze UI for MyGo](../breeze-ui.md).

## Looks

## Sources

`ComboRow`, `EntryRow`, `SpinRow` and `ExpanderRow` (`widgets_settings.go`, with `TestScreenshotsSettings`) complete the settings rows: they are built on `ActionRow`'s row look and join a `Card`, a `Fieldset` or an `AccordionItem` as it does. A combo row is `ui.SelectBase` (on GNOME the whole row is its trigger, as AdwComboRow activates on the row; on macOS and Windows its trigger is the control at the row's end, as SwiftUI and WinUI focus that control); an entry row holds a `ui.TextInputBase` named by the title; a spin row is `NumberInput`'s builder with its text named by the title and its buttons out of the Tab order, as WinUI's NumberBox and GTK's spin buttons are; an expander row is `ui.CollapsibleBase`, its header drawn as the platform's (AdwExpanderRow's 54 pixels and SettingsExpander's 70 DIPs, where `AccordionItem` keeps MyGo's shorter header), its panel holding rows as an `AccordionItem`'s do. Their values come from:

- **macOS:** SwiftUI's grouped `Form` holding a `Picker` of the menu style, `TextField`s with a label, a `Stepper` with a format and `DisclosureGroup`s whose label is a title over a subtitle, which `appkit-reference`'s settings scene renders at 2x in Aqua and Dark Aqua on macOS 27.2 where `TestScreenshotsSettings` places Breeze UI's rows (`appkit-settings-*.png`); Breeze UI's rows match it but for the text (the host's font) and the disabled row (see Limits). The comparison set the rows' heights: SwiftUI makes a picker's row of one line 37.5 points high and a stepper's 40.5 for their controls, where a row of text is 36.5, a text field's row half a point shorter than a row of text, and a closed disclosure group's header 44. The picker's circle is the section's separator share over the section (228 over 247, 54 over 37) and its chevrons labelColor over the circle; the stepper's field is `macField`'s bezel with its line laid over the section (210 over 247, 57 over 37), 9 points from the row's end, its stepper without a face, its line black at 12.5% (white at 13.8%) over the field. Still different: in Dark Aqua the render draws a disclosure group's chevron white, where Breeze UI keeps the disclosure button's white at 43.6% that `Collapsible` was measured with; whether that is SwiftUI's or the offscreen render's is not settled. The pop-up button's padding before the choice, the focus ring around the text field of an entry row and the picker's pressed look are inferred: the render shows none of them.
- **Windows 11:** CommunityToolkit/Windows's `SettingsCard.xaml` (SettingsCardContentMinWidth 120, which it gives a ComboBox and a TextBox in a card) and `SettingsExpander.xaml` (SettingsExpanderHeaderPadding 16,16,4,16, the 32-DIP ExpandCollapseChevronBorder with a Margin of 0,0,8,0 holding a 16-DIP AnimatedIcon of AnimatedChevronUpDownSmallVisualSource, the header's ToggleButton states, ExpanderContentDownBorderThickness 1,0,1,1 for the content, and SettingsExpanderItemPadding 58,8,44,8) at main a784bad, with breeze's `Select`, `TextInput` and `NumberInput` for the ComboBox, the TextBox and the NumberBox. The SettingsExpander is also compared with the one `winui-rows` renders: its header is 70 DIPs, 68 of fill inside its border, one line between it and the first item, which the content's -2 top margin lays over the header's bottom border; open, the header is `ControlFillColorDefault` (45 in the dark theme), which the ToggleButton keeps as its Checked state sets no fill, and closed `SettingsCardBackground` (43); the content has no fill of its own and the items are SettingsCards on the card's fill (251 and 43). [`tools/winui-reference`](../../tools/winui-reference/Program.cs)'s `winui-settings` scene renders SettingsCards holding a ComboBox, TextBoxes and NumberBoxes with inline spin buttons, and SettingsExpanders, open with a description and closed, where `TestScreenshotsSettings` places Breeze UI's rows, at 2x on Windows 11 26H2 with WinUI 3 3.3 and SettingsControls 8.2.251219, light and dark. Breeze UI's rows match it but for the text and the disabled row: the rows of a card 300 wide put their control 8 below the header and description at their start, 8 above their end, the ComboBox and the TextBox 120 wide, the NumberBox 156 (its 80-DIP text and the spin buttons, Up 4 after the text and Down 4 after Up, 32 by 24, 4 from the box's end), the expander's header 70 DIPs with the chevron's 32-DIP button 8 from its end, its fill 45 in the dark theme while open, its items 58 in, a ToggleSwitch with its Off text below an item's header. The comparison corrected what a SettingsCard holds, which WinUI draws translucent over the card's fill rather than over the window: the description, `TextFillColorSecondary`, reads 95 and 207 (92 and 204 over the window), a TextBox 254 inside a 236 border with a 139 bottom edge (55, 58 and 159 in the dark theme), a ComboBox, a NumberBox and a Button 254 and 55, and the line between two rows of a `Card` 236 and 39, `CardStrokeColorDefault` over the card, where a SettingsExpander's items lay theirs over its content, 229 and 29 (`TestFluentSettingsRowsOverTheCard`); the dark theme's translucent fills now take their exact alphas (`ControlFillColorDefault` #0FFFFFFF reads 55 over 43, not 56). It also corrected a disabled TextBox's and NumberBox's bottom edge, the border's color all round without the strong line. Still different: the text's line height, which makes WinUI's rows a few DIPs taller, and the disabled row, which WinUI keeps at full strength with its text in `TextFillColorDisabled` (160 and 126), its box `ControlFillColorDisabled` (250 and 52) and in the dark theme its spin buttons on a face of 61, where MyGo fades the whole row to half opacity (see Limits).
- **GNOME:** libadwaita 1.10.0's `adw-combo-row.ui` and `adw-combo-row.c` (the value an inline list view's label, the pan-down arrow box, the popover's 120-pixel least width, has-open-popup, the combo-box role), `adw-entry-row.ui` and `adw-entry-row.c` (allocate_editable_area: the title's scale and offset, TITLE_SPACING 3, EMPTY_ANIMATION_DURATION 150, the edit icon shown while not editing, pressed_cb focusing the text), `adw-spin-row.ui` and `adw-spin-row.c`, `adw-expander-row.ui`, `_lists.scss` (row.entry's header margin of 6 and editable area's padding of 6, .edit-icon's 24 and 5, row.spin's circular 30-pixel buttons with margin 10px 2px and border-spacing 6, the focus ring on .focused, row.combo, row.expander's arrow and nested list), `_spin-button.scss`, `_buttons.scss` (%circular_button) and the icons `adw-entry-edit.svg` and `adw-expander-arrow.svg`, whose strokes Breeze UI draws. The expander row is also compared with the AdwExpanderRow of `reference.py`'s `rows` set: its header, lines and nested list match, and its open arrow is `accent_color` as it renders, #0461be and #81d0ff, where a link's text reads a lighter #357fca. [`reference.py`](../../tools/adwaita-reference/reference.py)'s `settings` set renders an AdwComboRow with a subtitle, AdwEntryRows with and without text, an AdwSpinRow with a subtitle, an AdwComboRow and a disabled AdwSpinRow alone, and open and closed AdwExpanderRows where `TestScreenshotsSettings` places Breeze UI's rows, light and dark, with GTK 4.24.1, and Breeze UI's match it but for the glyphs: the rows' heights and lines, the combo row's choice and arrow, the entry rows' pencil and the empty row's centered title, the spin row's value and its 30-pixel buttons (235 and 72), and the expander rows. The comparison corrected the filled entry row's title, which Breeze UI drew as a small span on a line of the body's size, 3.5 pixels below libadwaita's, and now shapes at its own size where its small text is laid out; and the spin row's − and +, which libadwaita draws in the button's color over the button's face (47 over 235), where Breeze UI drew them over the card (51); the spin row's value, which GtkSpinButton draws in tabular digits (`.numeric`, `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`), the "1" of "12" 3 pixels further from the "2" than proportional digits put it, as `NumberInput`'s now is on GNOME too (the "10" of the `forms` set); and the + of a disabled button, which MyGo's half opacity, applied fill by fill, darkened where its two bars cross, where libadwaita draws it even (`reference.py`'s `spinlimit` set: an AdwSpinRow at its upper limit, its + 151 over the face of 245, 153 over 61 dark). GtkSpinButton gives its text the focus when one of its buttons is pressed, whatever had it (`button_pressed_cb` in GTK 4.24.1's `gtkspinbutton.c`), and so does the spin row's. Still different: the disabled spin row alone fades its card (see Limits); a disabled button's face and icon are halved one by one where GTK fades the button as a whole, so the + at the limit reads 146 over 245 to libadwaita's 151 (159 over 62 to 153 dark); and rendered on Linux with Adwaita Sans the filled entry row's title sits where libadwaita's does but its text 1.5 pixels lower, as GtkText puts its baseline on a whole pixel and its layout a whole pixel above it (`get_layout_position` in GTK 4.24.1's `gtktext.c`), and MyGo lays a line of Adwaita Sans out 19 pixels high where GTK measures 18; rendered on a Mac the host's font moves the text further.

## Limits

Besides [those of every widget](../breeze-ui.md#limits):

- **Settings rows of a later round.** `ComboRow`, `EntryRow`, `SpinRow` and `ExpanderRow` return the row, as `ActionRow` does, so that `Disabled` dims the whole row: Changed reports a choice, an edit or a new value, but an entry row's Submitted is not reachable, nor its field's `Password`. AdwPasswordEntryRow is left out: its peek button would need `PasswordField`'s per-platform reveal inside the row. The GNOME combo row's popover is aligned with the row's end, where GTK centers it on the arrow, and the macOS and Windows lists are `Select`'s. An entry row's text on Windows is 120 DIPs wide, the least SettingsCard gives a TextBox, where WinUI's TextBox grows with its text; on GNOME a spin row's text is 64 pixels wide, where GtkSpinButton fills the room the title leaves, which shows the same. GNOME draws an entry row's title shrinking as it scales the label, where Breeze UI draws it at the sizes in between. On Windows they are compared with WinUI's render (see Windows 11 above). On GNOME the rows are compared with libadwaita's render, but not what shows only under the pointer, pressed, focused or with the combo row's popover open. `NumberInput` on GNOME keeps `ui.NumberInput`'s buttons as Tab stops that take the focus when clicked, where GtkSpinButton's buttons take none and give it to the text. On Windows the controls a row holds are built in a theme laid over the row's fill (`ui.Themed`), so a `Select`'s list opened from a row takes it too: its edge and its items' fills are laid over the card's fill rather than the window's, which was not compared.

## What MyGo would need

Besides [what every widget would need](../breeze-ui.md#what-mygo-would-need):

- **Settings rows.** A way to place a popup at an offset along its anchor (or `PopoverBase` with an anchor element other than the trigger), to center AdwComboRow's popover on its arrow; a Submitted that a row can pass on from the field inside it.
