# Header bars

The widgets of [`ui/breeze/widgets_headerbar.go`](../../ui/breeze/widgets_headerbar.go), whose catalog entries are [`ui/breeze/catalog/headerbar.json`](../../ui/breeze/catalog/headerbar.json). What every widget shares, its platforms, themes and limits, is in [Breeze UI for MyGo](../breeze-ui.md).

## Looks

| Widget | macOS | Windows 11 | GNOME |
|---|---|---|---|
| `HeaderBar` | a window's unified toolbar showing its title: 52 points high on the window's background, a half-point line along its bottom, the title 12 after the start controls in 15-point semibold, or 13-point bold over an 11-point secondary subtitle; over a content pane beside a sidebar, the first item 8 from the pane's edge, as after a tracking separator | TitleBar: 48 high with controls and 32 without, transparent, the title in the caption style 14 after the LeftHeader, the subtitle beside it, the RightHeader 48 before the caption buttons; over a pane, the LeftHeader at the pane's edge | AdwHeaderBar: at least 47 high on `headerbar_bg_color` with `headerbar_shade_color` along its bottom, the bold title centered across the bar, the subtitle under it smaller and dimmed; over a pane, the controls 6 from its edges and the title centered across it |
| `Button`, `IconButton`, `BackButton`, `ForwardButton` and the other buttons in a `HeaderBar` | as in a `Toolbar`: 36-point items, image items side by side sharing one platter | image buttons as the TitleBar's back button: 40 wide, as tall as the bar less 4, subtle; text buttons, toggles and menu buttons as outside a toolbar | as in a toolbar (AdwHeaderBar extends `%toolbar`): text buttons raised, image and menu buttons flat |

`HeaderBar(c, title, subtitle, start, end)` draws the title bar of a window whose title bar is hidden (`mygo.WindowOptions{TitleBarStyle: mygo.TitleBarHidden}`): the title and an optional subtitle, which macOS (`NSWindow.subtitle`), WinUI (`TitleBar.Subtitle`) and libadwaita (`AdwWindowTitle:subtitle`) all show, and the controls `start` and `end` build, `nil` for none. It keeps clear of the window controls in the room `c.TitleBar()` reports, is at least as high as they are, moves the window when dragged anywhere but over a control and maximizes it on a double click, as MyGo's `DragWindow` does; it is a group named by its title, as WinUI names its `TitleBar`. Each control in it is a Tab stop of its own. Put it first in the window's column, or first in the column of a pane beside a sidebar: it keeps clear of only the window controls it lies under, the part of `c.TitleBar()`'s room its own box reaches into, so over the content beside a sidebar that holds the traffic lights its controls begin 8 points from the pane's edge on macOS (a unified toolbar's items after a tracking separator over a split view's divider), 2 DIPs in on Windows (a TitleBar with no caption buttons at its start) and 6 pixels in on GNOME, its title centered across the pane (a content pane's AdwHeaderBar without window buttons at its start), and a bar below the controls' band keeps clear of none. On macOS give the traffic lights `TrafficLightPosition: &mygo.Point{X: 19, Y: 19}`, where AppKit puts them in a unified toolbar, and on Windows `TitleBarHeight: 48`, so that the caption buttons are as tall as a bar with controls.

## Sources

`HeaderBar` (`widgets_headerbar.go`, with `TestScreenshotsHeaderBar` and `TestScreenshotsHeaderBarInPane`) is not one of MyGo's widgets: it is a row built of MyGo's elements that moves the window (`Element.DragWindow`), sized from the room `Context.TitleBar` reports. Its values come from:

- **macOS:** a titled window with a unified toolbar showing its title (and `NSWindow.subtitle`), with a navigational back item before the title and a bordered text item and image item after a flexible space, rendered at 2x into an sRGB bitmap on macOS 27.2 by the header bar scenes of [`tools/appkit-reference`](../../tools/appkit-reference), which print the traffic lights', the title fields' and the platters' frames and the title's font and color: the bar is 52 points high, the traffic lights 14 points square 19 points down and 19, 42 and 65 points in, the first item 17 points after the zoom button, the title 12 after the item before it (29 after the zoom button without one) in `.SFNS-Semibold` 15 points and `labelColor`, or with a subtitle in `.SFNS-Bold` 13 over `.SFNS-Regular` 11 in `secondaryLabelColor`, the last item 8 from the window's edge, and a half-point line below the bar, black at 10.2% over white and white at 7.1% over the dark window. Its split header bar scenes render the same toolbar over an `NSSplitViewController` whose 236-point sidebar holds the traffic lights, the items and the title after an `NSTrackingSeparatorToolbarItem` over the divider, with and without the back item and the subtitle: there the back item's platter is 8 points after the divider, the title 12 after it (20 after the divider without the item), the last item 8 from the window's edge and the line under the content pane alone, as Breeze UI's bar over a pane beside a sidebar lays them out (`TestHeaderBarInPaneKeepsClearOfTheControlsOverIt`, `TestScreenshotsHeaderBarInPane`).
- **Windows 11:** WinUI's `TitleBar` control on microsoft-ui-xaml's main branch (`controls/dev/TitleBar`): `TitleBar.xaml`'s columns (the caption buttons' padding columns, the back and pane toggle buttons, the LeftHeader, `TitleBarLeftHeaderPaddingWidth` 14, the title and subtitle in `CaptionTextBlockStyle` with margins of 8 and 16, the content, the RightHeader and `TitleBarMinDragRegionWidth` 48), `TitleBar_themeresources.xaml` (`TitleBarCompactHeight` 32, `TitleBarExpandedHeight` 48, `TitleBarBackButtonStyle`: 40 wide, a margin of 2, subtle fills), `TitleBar.cpp` (the height by whether a header or content is set, the padding columns from `AppWindowTitleBar`'s insets) and `TitleBarAutomationPeer.cpp` (the bar named by its title). MyGo's Windows host draws the caption buttons, 46 DIPs wide each, over the window's top right corner (`internal/windows/titlebar.go`), so Breeze UI draws none. [`tools/winui-reference`](../../tools/winui-reference/Program.cs)'s `winui-headerbar` and `winui-headerbar-subtitle` scenes render a TitleBar 422 DIPs wide (the window less MyGo's three caption buttons), light and dark at 2x on Windows 11 26H2, with its own back button, Documents and 12 items, and a RightHeader holding a Button reading Edit and a subtle 40 by 44 Share button 4 after it; Breeze UI's bar matches it but for the text (the host's font) and the icons (the test's SVGs): the bar is 48 high and transparent over the window, its back button 40 by 44 two DIPs in, the title and subtitle in the caption style (26 and 92 over 243, 255 and 204 over 32), vertically centered, the subtitle 8 after the title, the RightHeader ending 48 DIPs before the caption buttons and its image button 40 by 44 two DIPs inside it, and the content 16 below the bar. Still different: WinUI puts its title 2 DIPs after its own back button's column, 4 after the button's face (the title at 46; `NegativeInsetSpacing`, set by `TitleBar::UpdateLeftHeaderSpacing` while one of the back and pane toggle buttons shows), where Breeze UI keeps the LeftHeader's 14 after whatever `start` holds (16 after an image button's face), as the scene's first render, with a Button of TitleBarBackButtonStyle as the LeftHeader, showed (the title at 58); that Button is only 16 DIPs high there, the LeftHeader being centered, where Breeze UI gives an image button in `start` the back button's 44 (see Limits).
- **GNOME:** libadwaita 1.10.0's `_header-bar.scss` (the bar's 47-pixel minimum height, its box padded 6, 7, 7 and 7, the start and end boxes' spacing and margins, AdwWindowTitle's `.title` and `.subtitle`), `adw-window-title.ui` (the title label's width-chars of 5: GtkLabel asks for 5 of Pango's approximate character widths, the widest digit's in Adwaita Sans Bold, 10, so a short title's label is wider than its text), `_toolbars.scss` (`%toolbar`, which makes image and menu buttons flat and leaves text buttons raised) and `_colors.scss` (`headerbar_bg_color`, `headerbar_shade_color`), and GTK 4.24.1's `font-size: smaller`, the size divided by 1.2 (`gtkcssenumvalue.c`). GTK draws the window buttons in a GTK 3 header bar of its own over the window (MyGo's `internal/linux/titlebar.go`), so Breeze UI draws none. [`tools/adwaita-reference`](../../tools/adwaita-reference/reference.py)'s sets `headerbar` and `headerbarsub` render an AdwHeaderBar at the top of the window's content, with GTK's close button at its end (`decoration-layout` `:close`), light and dark, with GTK 4.24.1, and Breeze UI's bar matches it but for the glyphs and the test's icons: 47 pixels high, its fill (255 and 46) and its shade in the last row (224 and 29), the 34-pixel buttons 6 below its top, the raised Edit button's face (235) and the title centered across the bar. The comparison corrected the controls' inset: the bar's margins of -1px at its sides put it a pixel past the window's edges, so its padding of 7 leaves the controls 6 from them (the back button at 6), where Breeze UI put them 7 in. The room of 46 pixels that `TestScreenshotsHeaderBar` gives the window controls, inferred from MyGo's GTK 3 header bar, ends the end controls at 514 in the 560-pixel window, where libadwaita ends them, 6 before its close button (520 to 554). Rendered on Linux with Adwaita Sans, as GTK draws it, the title sits half a pixel lower than libadwaita's (a pixel with the subtitle, which is a pixel lower too) and the Edit button is 61 pixels wide to GTK's 62: MyGo lays a line of Adwaita Sans at 11 points out 19 pixels high where GTK's labels take 18, and measures "Edit" a pixel narrower; rendered on a Mac the host's font moves the text further.

## Limits

Besides [those of every widget](../breeze-ui.md#limits):

- **Header bars.** `HeaderBar` draws no window controls: the traffic lights are AppKit's, the caption buttons MyGo's and the window buttons GTK's, and on macOS they stay where MyGo's hidden title bar puts them unless `TrafficLightPosition` moves them. AppKit draws the line under its toolbar half a point below the 52-point bar; Breeze UI draws it inside the bar's bottom edge, so that the content's background does not cover it, and it was measured offscreen. The room a bar keeps clear of comes from where the previous frame laid it out: a new bar is taken to run along the window's top from edge to edge, and a bar drawn where it lies under other room than it kept clear of, as when the sidebar beside it comes or goes, asks for another frame, so for one frame after it moves its controls are where they were in the old place; the bar asks for it from a `DrawOver` callback of its own, which a `DrawOver` given to the bar replaces. Breeze UI does not draw the sidebar, the divider or a tracking separator: the bar starts where its pane does. The macOS layout over a pane is compared with AppKit's split header bar scenes; the Windows and GNOME ones are inferred from WinUI's TitleBar (no LeftPaddingColumn without caption buttons at the start) and libadwaita's AdwHeaderBar (no window buttons at a side the window's controls are not on), not rendered. Controls that do not fit are not moved into an overflow menu, as a `Toolbar`'s are, and on GNOME each side is at least as wide as its controls were in the previous frame, so a side that grows is laid out once more. WinUI's TitleBar icon, its own back and pane toggle buttons and its Content are not drawn: put a `BackButton` or an `IconButton` in `start`, which take the back button's look, 40 by 44 DIPs; the title follows `start` 14 DIPs after it, as after a LeftHeader, where WinUI puts it 2 after the column of its own back button or pane toggle button shown alone, 4 after the button's face (`TitleBar::UpdateLeftHeaderSpacing`), and a WinUI app's own button in the LeftHeader is only as tall as its content. libadwaita's flat header bar of an AdwToolbarView (46 pixels, transparent) is not drawn. `DragWindow` cannot be observed with `ui.Tester`, whose host does nothing on a window drag, so the tests check that a press on the bar clicks and focuses nothing rather than that it moves the window.
