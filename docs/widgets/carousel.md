# Pagers and carousels

The widgets of [`ui/breeze/widgets_carousel.go`](../../ui/breeze/widgets_carousel.go), whose catalog entries are [`ui/breeze/catalog/carousel.json`](../../ui/breeze/catalog/carousel.json). What every widget shares, its platforms, themes and limits, is in [Breeze UI for MyGo](../breeze-ui.md).

## Looks

| Widget | macOS | Windows 11 | GNOME |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PipsPager` | UIPageControl as UIKit for Mac draws it (macOS has no page indicator; inferred): 6-point dots 19 apart in a 23-point control, the chosen one black (white in Dark Aqua), the others at 45% | PipsPager: 12 by 24 pips, a 4-DIP dot of ControlStrongFillColorDefault, the chosen one 6, a pip under the pointer 6 in TextFillColorSecondary; at most 5 shown, scrolled to keep the chosen one in the middle; no previous or next button, as by default | AdwCarouselIndicatorDots: dots 15 apart, of radius 3 at 30% of the text's color, the chosen one of radius 4 at 90%, changing gradually as the choice moves |
| `Carousel` | the pages sliding as NSPageController's horizontal strip moves them, a touchpad's swipe moving them with the fingers, UIPageControl's dots right below (inferred) | FlipView: previous and next buttons 16 by 38 at its sides while the mouse moves over it, a notch of the wheel flipping a page, an adjacent page sliding in and another appearing at once; a PipsPager centered 10 below | AdwCarousel: a touchpad's swipe or a drag moving the pages, libadwaita's spring between them, a notch of the wheel moving a page; AdwCarouselIndicatorDots below with a margin of 6, following the pages as they move |

## Sources

`PipsPager` and `Carousel` (`widgets_carousel.go`, with `TestScreenshotsCarousel`) have no counterpart in package `ui`. A pager is a row of page indicators choosing `*selected` of `count` pages; a carousel shows the page that its function builds for `*selected`, building the pages coming into view as it moves and no others, each in a container keyed by its index, with the platform's dots below it. Each is one Tab stop, none without pages, as a PipsPager without pips and a FlipView or AdwCarousel without pages have nothing to focus: the pager a radio group named Pager whose radio buttons, its pips, are named Page 1, Page 2, as WinUI names its pips (`PipsPagerNameText`, `PipsPagerPageText`), the one chosen being its active descendant; the carousel its view, named by the page shown ("Page 2 of 4"), its dots no Tab stop. The pager's arrows choose the page before or after, Left and Right mirrored right to left, Up and Down as WinUI's PipsPager takes them, stopping at the ends; the carousel's arrows, Page Up, Page Down, Home and End move as AdwCarousel's bindings and WinUI's Selector take them, Up and Down on Windows only. MyGo lets a view take a touchpad's scrolling (`InputScroll` with `Precise` and `Momentum`, through `HandleInput`), a drag of the pointer (`Dragged`, `DragEnded`) and animate a value with a spring or a curve (`AnimateTo`, `Jump`), so the pages follow a swipe and move as each platform moves them. Their values come from:

- **macOS:** AppKit has no page indicator and no carousel with one: the macOS 27.2 SDK's SwiftUI marks `PageTabViewStyle` and `IndexViewStyle` `@available(macOS, unavailable)`, and AppKit's NSPageController, which swipes between pages, shows no dots. The look is inferred from what macOS does draw: UIKit's UIPageControl in UIKit for Mac (a Mac Catalyst app in the Mac idiom), which SwiftUI's page `TabView` puts below its pages there. [`pagecontrol.swift`](../../tools/appkit-reference/pagecontrol.swift), a Mac Catalyst app, renders UIPageControls at 2x on macOS 27.2 where `TestScreenshotsCarousel` places Breeze UI's pagers, and the carousel's below its 320 by 160 view, light and dark (`uikit-pagecontrol-*.png`), and a SwiftUI page `TabView` alone, printing their frames: indicator views 7 points square and 19 apart, each drawing a circle 6 points across in its middle, in a control 23 points high with 14 points from either end to the first and the last (111 points wide for 5 pages, 187 for 9), the chosen dot white and the others white at 45% (131 over the dark window's 30, 115 over black), in either appearance; in the page `TabView` the control stands 12 points above the view's bottom. Breeze UI's dots, compared pixel by pixel with that render in Dark Aqua, stand on the same pixels with the same fills, differing only in the antialiasing of their rims (by up to 37 levels on a few pixels at each dot's corners). UIKit's white dots do not show in a light window, so in Aqua Breeze UI draws them black, the others at 45% (inferred). The carousel's pages slide as NSPageController's horizontal strip moves them, over 0.25 seconds, NSAnimationContext's default (inferred); `appkit-reference`'s carousel scene renders an NSPageController of that style showing the second of four pages where the test places Breeze UI's view (`appkit-carousel-*.png`). A touchpad's swipe moves the pages with the fingers, a page to the view's width, and the mouse wheel moves none, as NSPageController follows only a touchpad (inferred); the dots stand right below the view, centered (inferred), and AppKit's focus ring goes around the view or the pager, round at the pager's ends (inferred).

## Limits

Besides [those of every widget](../breeze-ui.md#limits):

- **Pagers and carousels.** macOS has no page indicator, so its look is UIKit for Mac's UIPageControl, in black in Aqua where UIKit draws white (inferred); its background platter, which UIPageControl shows while it is used (`backgroundStyle` automatic), is not drawn. WinUI's PipsPager moves the focus among its pips with the arrows, each pip a button that Space or a click chooses, and takes Tab at the previous button when shown; Breeze UI keeps one Tab stop whose arrows choose, as MyGo's radio groups do. Its previous and next buttons (`PreviousButtonVisibility` and `NextButtonVisibility`, collapsed by default), its wrap mode, its vertical orientation and a `MaxVisiblePips` other than 5 are not provided; nor a carousel's vertical orientation, spacing between pages, long swipes or AdwCarouselIndicatorLines. AdwCarouselIndicatorDots takes no clicks and no focus and names nothing; Breeze UI's dots take clicks, and alone the keys, so that a pager without a carousel can be used, with the focus ring inside its edge (inferred). In WinUI the PipsPager below a FlipView is a Tab stop of its own; in a `Carousel` the dots are not, its view taking the keys. A carousel's height is the shown page's, and the pages moving into view's while they move, where AdwCarousel asks for its tallest page's, as the pages not shown are not built. MyGo reports no scroll phases, so a touchpad's swipe ends with its momentum, which is then dropped, or after 150 ms without a move, where GTK reads the scroll's stop event, and it reports no pointer type, so FlipView's buttons show for any pointer, where WinUI hides them for a touch, and a touch's pan of the FlipView and a precision touchpad's DirectManipulation pan, which follow the fingers in WinUI, flip a page per gesture as a wheel's notches do. The curves of WinUI's moves between pages and of its pips' scrolling are inferred. AdwCarousel starts its spring again from rest when a key or the wheel sends the pages elsewhere as they move (`scroll_to` with no velocity) and clamps it, so that it stops at the page rather than going past it; MyGo's spring keeps the value's velocity when its target changes and is not clamped, so the pages keep their speed into a new move and may go past the page by a hair after a quick swipe ending near it. A disabled pager or carousel takes MyGo's half opacity, where WinUI draws the pips in `ControlStrongFillColorDisabled`.

## What MyGo would need

Besides [what every widget would need](../breeze-ui.md#what-mygo-would-need):

- **Pagers and carousels.** The phase of a touchpad's scrolling in `InputEvent` (began, changed, ended, as NSEvent's phase and GDK's stop event report it), so that a swipe ends when the fingers leave rather than after a pause; the pointer's type (mouse, pen, touch) in `InputEvent` and for `Hovered`, so that FlipView's buttons hide for a touch and a touch pans the pages; a way to measure an element without showing it, so that a carousel takes its tallest page's height without building every page into view; and a spring that starts from rest when its target changes and one clamped at its target (AdwSpringAnimation's `clamp`), as AdwCarousel's is.
